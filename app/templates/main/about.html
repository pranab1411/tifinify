The Problem We Solve

For students, interns, and young professionals relocating to educational hubs, finding healthy, dependable daily tiffin and mess food is critical. Traditional rating platforms show aggregate stars (e.g. 4.5/5), but star ratings are susceptible to manipulation via promotional bursts, copy-paste reviews, or biased family-and-friends submissions.

Tifinify goes beyond "What is the star rating?" by asking: "What are customers actually saying, how consistent are their opinions, and are there detectable suspicious linguistic patterns?"

Review Intelligence Pipeline

Text Preprocessing

Normalizes whitespace, cleans unicode characters, strips HTML tags, and ensures clean text representations.

Near-Duplicate Detection

Computes word n-gram TF-IDF vectors and pairwise Cosine Similarity matrix to detect copy-paste phrasing (≥ 0.82 similarity).

Behavioral Heuristics

Identifies rating-sentiment contradictions (e.g., 1-star with glowing praise), promotional jargon, and temporal bursts.

Aspect-Based Analysis

Extracts sentiment on Taste, Hygiene, Food Quality, Quantity, Delivery, and Value for Money across all reviews.

Review Trust Score Formula

The Review Trust Score is calculated using a deterministic, transparent formula that evaluates statistical anomalies:

Trust Score = 100 - min(100, (P_duplicate × 0.35 + P_inconsistency × 0.30 + P_burst × 0.20 + P_polarization × 0.15))
🟢 Likely Authentic
80 – 100
Natural linguistic diversity & regular intervals.
🟡 Potentially Suspicious
50 – 79
Minor phrase repetition or rating mismatches.
🔴 Highly Suspicious
0 – 49
Strong near-duplicate clusters or severe bursts.
Responsible AI Principle & Disclaimer

Tifinify does not assert that an individual review is definitively fraudulent. The system evaluates algorithmic patterns, linguistic similarity, and statistical anomalies in available text data. Consumers should use these indicators alongside personal inquiries to make their meal decisions.
