1. PREFACE

The rapid migration of students, interns, and young working professionals toward major educational and industrial centers has fundamentally transformed the demand for daily meal solutions. Unlike occasional restaurant dining, subscribing to a local mess or daily tiffin delivery service represents an essential health and financial commitment. Consumers depend upon their meal provider twice a day, every day, for nutritional balance, digestive hygiene, and predictable delivery.

Consequently, consumer decision-making relies almost entirely on online customer feedback and ratings. However, contemporary commercial search directories and mapping platforms suffer from severe vulnerabilities: arithmetic star ratings (e.g. 4.6/5) are routinely distorted by paid 5-star review campaigns, copy-pasted marketing text, coordinated review bursts, and automated bot submissions. Conversely, authentic consumer warnings regarding poor sanitation, food poisoning, or erratic delivery are easily masked by inflated aggregates.

Tifinify has been conceptualized and engineered as an integrated Master of Computer Application software project to solve this exact trust deficit. Rather than functioning as a passive business catalog, Tifinify unites modern web engineering, spatial algorithms, natural language processing, machine learning heuristics, grounded generative AI, and responsive web design into an objective consumer decision-support ecosystem.

The defining engineering principle of Tifinify is Explainable Review Intelligence & Trust Auditing. The platform explicitly separates external star ratings from an objective, mathematically derived Review Trust Score (0–100). Using TF-IDF n-gram vectorization, pairwise Cosine Similarity (for duplicate detection), sentiment-rating inconsistency checks, temporal burst heuristics, and Google Gemini AI contextual synthesis with real web grounding, Tifinify empowers users with transparent evaluations.

Additionally, Tifinify exhibits strong architectural resilience. While Google Gemini delivers rich contextual summaries, the system incorporates deterministic local NLP heuristics and zero-billing web search intelligence (DuckDuckGo and OpenStreetMap Overpass API) as a primary operational pipeline. This guarantees uninterrupted real business discovery even without paid external commercial API keys.

2. INTRODUCTION

Finding hygienic, affordable, and dependable food upon relocating to an unfamiliar city is one of the most stressful challenges facing university students and junior professionals. While commercial food delivery giants cater to high-margin restaurant orders, local tiffin kitchens and student messes operate in an unorganized sector characterized by zero regulatory transparency and high review manipulation.

Tifinify bridges this gap by transforming local meal discovery into an intelligent, guided web workflow. Users can explore verified providers within customizable geographic radiuses (1 km, 3 km, 5 km, 10 km), inspect interactive spatial maps, contrast monthly subscription plans, and examine an explainable AI trust audit distinguishing genuine community experiences from deceptive promotional spam.

2.1 Background

Mainstream search engines and directory platforms aggregate numerical star ratings through simple arithmetic averaging. This naive model fails to analyze review credibility, text uniqueness, or temporal distribution. A vendor with 40 manufactured 5-star blurbs posted in 48 hours easily outranks a genuine home cook with 20 balanced, authentic reviews. Tifinify addresses this through a hybrid AI intelligence pipeline combining statistical machine learning with generative language models.

2.2 Problem Statement

Students and young professionals lack a reliable, objective system to discover authentic local tiffin and mess providers, evaluate actual food hygiene and taste, and detect manipulated online ratings. The problem addressed by Tifinify is to design and develop an intelligent, location-based web application that discovers genuine physical food establishments, audits customer reviews through machine learning heuristics, computes an explainable Review Trust Score (0–100), extracts multi-aspect sentiment, and presents findings through an accessible, dark-mode-enabled interface.

2.3 Significance of the Project

The significance of Tifinify lies in demonstrating that responsible, explainable Artificial Intelligence can be seamlessly integrated with full-stack web software. The project integrates multi-radius spatial filtering, TF-IDF cosine similarity matrices, grounded Gemini AI synthesis, SQLite relational storage, Leaflet mapping, and responsive web design into an open-source, zero-billing architecture.

3. SCOPE AND OBJECTIVES OF THE SYSTEM

The scope of Tifinify is focused on individual food discovery, review authenticity verification, and consumer decision support for daily recurring meal services. The system processes voluntary customer feedback, public web directory listings, and geographic coordinates to deliver structured analytical findings. The scope does not include kitchen inventory management, order cooking, or payment escrow.

Objectives

  • Multi-Radius Spatial Search: Enable location-based discovery across 1 km, 3 km, 5 km, and 10 km boundaries using the Haversine trigonometric formula.
  • Zero-Billing Place Discovery: Implement real-world search intelligence adapters (DuckDuckGo, Google Search index, OpenStreetMap Overpass API) to locate authentic operational businesses without commercial API costs.
  • AI Review Intelligence: Integrate Google Gemini AI (`google-genai` SDK) with real search grounding to generate objective, 2-3 sentence summaries of customer experiences.
  • Deterministic NLP Trust Auditing: Formulate a 4-pillar MCDA Trust Score based on text uniqueness (TF-IDF Cosine Similarity), rating-sentiment consistency, and burst heuristics.
  • Aspect-Based Sentiment Breakdown: Extract sentiment across five key food service dimensions: Taste, Hygiene, Food Quality, Delivery, and Value for Money.
  • Rating Separation & Badging: Clearly distinguish raw external star ratings (e.g. ⭐ 4.5/5) from Tifinify's AI Review Trust Score (e.g. 🛡 92/100).
  • Geospatial Map Visualization: Plot meal providers on interactive Leaflet.js maps with custom category pins (Veg, Non-Veg, Both, Jain).
  • Accessible Modern UI: Deliver a sleek food-tech interface featuring a persistent, zero-flicker Dark/Light theme mode.
  • Objective Comparison Tool: Provide a side-by-side tabular comparison module contrasting distance, monthly pricing, aspect satisfaction, and trust scores.
  • Secure Authentication & Storage: Manage user preferences, favorites, and accounts using salted Werkzeug password hashing and relational SQLite storage.

The project is implemented using modern, stable technologies: Python 3.11+, Flask 3.x, SQLAlchemy 2.0 ORM, `scikit-learn`, and Bootstrap 5. The interface is clean, mobile-responsive, and intuitive. Users can detect their location with a single click or search any locality. Mathematical trust scores are simplified into intuitive badges (Likely Authentic, Potentially Suspicious, Highly Suspicious). 4.4 Schedule Feasibility

Development was structured across modular, agile milestones: schema design, spatial search engine, TF-IDF trust algorithms, Gemini AI integration, real-place search adapters, UI/dark mode engineering, and automated testing. All phases were completed systematically within the semester schedule.

4.5 Ethical and Privacy Feasibility

Tifinify adheres strictly to data ethics. User geolocation is ephemeral and never tracked. User credentials are encrypted with cryptographic salts. Scraped listings represent public commercial establishments. The trust algorithm operates on transparent, explainable heuristics rather than arbitrary vendor discrimination.

6. TECHNOLOGIES USED

The technology stack in Tifinify was chosen to achieve high performance, clean architectural separation, and zero operational overhead.

6.1 Python

Python serves as the primary programming language, executing routing, trigonometric calculations, machine learning pipelines, and database operations within a cohesive ecosystem.

6.2 Flask Web Framework

Flask 3.x provides the core web architecture. Using the Application Factory pattern (`create_app`) and Blueprints (`main_bp`, `provider_bp`, `auth_bp`, `api_bp`, `admin_bp`), the application maintains clean separation between routing, business logic, and RESTful APIs.

6.3 Google Gemini AI & Grounded Search

Google Gemini (`gemini-2.5-flash`) acts as the contextual intelligence layer. When customer reviews are ingested, Gemini synthesizes grounded, objective summaries. Furthermore, with Google Search grounding enabled, Gemini identifies operational food services in any target city.

6.4 NLP & Scikit-Learn Heuristic Engine

The review trust pipeline uses `scikit-learn` to vectorize review texts into n-gram TF-IDF matrices. Pairwise Cosine Similarity matrices uncover duplicate spam. Rule-based heuristics flag temporal bursts and sentiment-rating contradictions.

6.5 SQLite & SQLAlchemy ORM

SQLite provides an integrated relational database requiring zero external daemon management. SQLAlchemy 2.0 ORM manages relational integrity, migrations, cascading deletes, and optimized queries.

6.6 OpenMaps (Leaflet.js & OpenStreetMap Overpass)

Interactive maps are rendered client-side using Leaflet.js. Real business queries interface with OpenStreetMap's Overpass API (`overpass-api.de`) to discover registered canteens, messes, and eateries without requiring paid commercial mapping keys.

6.7 Supporting Technologies

Bootstrap 5.3.3 handles responsive design; Chart.js renders canvas sentiment distributions; Werkzeug manages password security; and Open-Meteo & IP-geolocation APIs provide automated coordinate resolution.

8. PROPOSED SYSTEM

Tifinify is architectured as a multi-tier web application adhering to MVC and service-layer patterns. Users search for an area or activate GPS geolocation. The system computes coordinates, runs Haversine spatial queries, and automatically executes live web search discovery if a new area is searched.

Every provider record displays two distinct, uncoupled metrics: raw customer star ratings (⭐ 4.6/5) and Tifinify's AI Review Trust Score (🛡 88/100). Users can view sentiment charts, read Gemini AI summaries, inspect aspect satisfaction, view map locations, and compare providers side-by-side.

8.1 Multi-Criteria Review Trust Evaluation Model

The Review Trust Score is computed through a 4-pillar Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) model:

  • Text Diversity & Uniqueness (35%): Penalizes duplicate phrasing identified via TF-IDF cosine similarity (>0.85 threshold).
  • Rating-Sentiment Consistency (25%): Detects contradictions between 5-star ratings and severe complaints (*"spoiled"*, *"hair in food"*, *"stale"*).
  • Temporal Burst Distribution (20%): Identifies unnatural review clusters published within short timeframes.
  • Reviewer Sample Volume & Profile Balance (20%): Evaluates sample size and distribution fairness.

8.2 Real Business Search Intelligence Pipeline

When searching an unseeded locality, `WebSearchPlacesAdapter` queries DuckDuckGo and Google search indices using targeted regex filters (`\b(tiffin|mess|rasoi|bhojanalaya|canteen)\b`) and queries OpenStreetMap Overpass with a 3-second timeout. Extracted establishments are sanitized, ingested, scored, and plotted.

8.3 AI and Grounded Fallback Flow

Primary Flow: Gemini AI generates contextual review summaries and discovers businesses via Google Search grounding.
Fallback Flow: If the Gemini API quota is exhausted or key omitted, the platform automatically activates deterministic local NLP heuristics and web scraping with zero interruption.

9. MODULES

Tifinify's codebase is strictly modularized into independent, cohesive functional modules:

9.1 Authentication & User Preferences Module

Handles user registration, login, session persistence, role authorization (Consumer vs. Administrator), dietary preferences, and search radius defaults. Passwords utilize salted Werkzeug hashing.

9.2 Spatial Discovery & Multi-Radius Search Module

Executes spherical Haversine trigonometric distance calculations, filtering providers within 1 km, 3 km, 5 km, and 10 km radiuses alongside category, dietary, and budget constraints.

9.3 Real-World Web & Overpass Search Intelligence Module

Integrates `WebSearchPlacesAdapter` to discover authentic operational providers from public web directories and OpenStreetMap Overpass servers without requiring paid Google Maps billing.

9.4 Gemini AI Contextual Review Intelligence Module

Utilizes Google Gemini (`google-genai` SDK) to synthesize concise, objective 2-3 sentence summaries of customer experiences, highlighting recurring praises and complaints.

9.5 TF-IDF Similarity & Duplicate Detection Engine

Converts reviews into n-gram TF-IDF matrices using `scikit-learn` and calculates pairwise Cosine Similarity. Pairs scoring above 0.85 are flagged as duplicate promotional spam.

9.6 Behavioral Heuristics & Sentiment Inconsistency Module

Identifies severe rating-text contradictions (e.g., 5.0 stars accompanied by terms like *"stale"*, *"unhygienic"*, or *"food poisoning"*).

9.7 Aspect-Based Extraction & Scoring Module

Extracts and quantifies sentiment across five vital dimensions: Taste, Hygiene, Food Quality, Delivery, and Value for Money.

9.8 Explainable Trust Badge & Rating Separation Module

Assigns normalized Trust Scores (0–100) and displays intuitive badges: 🛡 Likely Authentic (≥75), ⚠️ Potentially Suspicious (50–74), 🚨 Highly Suspicious (<50).

9.9 Side-by-Side Provider Comparison Module

Renders a tabular side-by-side comparison (`/providers/compare`) contrasting distance, pricing, aspect satisfaction, and review trust.

9.10 Interactive OpenMaps / Leaflet Mapping Module

Renders OpenStreetMap tiles via Leaflet.js with dynamic markers, radius circles, interactive popups, and automatic dark-mode tile styling.

9.11 Dark/Light Theme & UI State Engine

Delivers a theme switcher with an inline pre-hydration script in `` that reads `localStorage` and system settings, completely eliminating white page-load flicker.

9.12 Admin Governance & Telemetry Audit Module

Provides an administrative portal displaying system telemetry: registered users, active providers, reviews analyzed, flagged duplicate reviews, and audit logs.

9.13 Automated Testing & Quality Assurance Module

Houses the automated `pytest` suite containing 22 unit and integration tests verifying NLP heuristics, database integrity, and API routes.

11.1 Entity Description

  • Users: Stores user identity, authentication hashes, role (user/admin), and profile timestamps.
  • UserPreferences: 1-to-1 extension of Users storing personal budget, distance, and dietary preferences.
  • Providers: Master food service entity; contains business name, category (tiffin/mess), coordinates, monthly subscription rate, Trust Score, and trust badge.
  • ProviderSources: Tracks origin metadata (`web_search`, `gemini_discovery`, `osm_overpass`).
  • Reviews: Normalized customer reviews with numeric rating (1.0–5.0), author, date, and text.
  • ReviewAnalysis: 1-to-1 review audit storing sentiment labels, duplicate spam flags, and penalty reasons.
  • ReviewAspects: Stores aspect-level breakdown (Hygiene, Taste, Quality, Delivery, Price).
  • ProviderAnalysisSummary: Aggregates positive/negative sentiment shares and Gemini AI summary.
  • Favorites: Many-to-many relationship linking users to bookmarked providers.
  • Reports & AdminAuditLog: Audit trails recording user dispute reports and admin interventions.

15. DATABASE DETAILS AND DATA INTEGRITY

Tifinify uses SQLite 3 through SQLAlchemy 2.0 ORM, ensuring zero daemon overhead for local development and total portability to PostgreSQL for production deployments.

15.1 Data Integrity and Security

  • Foreign Key Cascades: Deleting a provider cascades deletion to reviews, aspect scores, and sources, preventing orphaned records.
  • Unique Constraints: Strict indexing on provider slugs and user emails prevents race-condition duplicates.
  • Parameterized Queries: SQLAlchemy compiles all statements into parameterized SQL, preventing SQL Injection.
  • Salted Password Hashing: Credentials utilize modern PBKDF2/scrypt cryptographic hashing.
  • Audit Lineage: Every record retains upstream source metadata (`web_search`, `gemini_discovery`).
